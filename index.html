The Heart of NLP4Ed

Who We Are

NLP4Ed is a small yet passionate team of tech enthusiasts, composed of EFL teachers and education researchers. Our mission is to bring the benefits of AI and natural language processing (NLP) advancements to English as a Foreign Language (EFL) education in Morocco. We believe in democratizing technology to enhance learning and teaching experiences for all.

Our Philosophy

We cater to both non-technical users and specialists. Whether you’re a teacher with no coding experience or a tech-savvy user, our community offers support tailored to different needs. Our goal is to make AI and NLP tools accessible to educators and to ensure that these technologies become mainstream in the Moroccan education sector.

Our Motivation

This community is for data lovers who believe in the power of quantitative analysis. Our aim is to provide support and foster an understanding of how data can drive impactful research in education. Together, we can make a difference in how education evolves in Morocco by harnessing the potential of AI and NLP technologies.

What We Aim to Achieve

Our mission is to revolutionize English language education in Morocco by leveraging AI and NLP technologies.

Develop Open-Source Tools for Education

Create and share open-source tools and scripts tailored to enhancing English language education through AI and NLP technologies.

Build a Collaborative Community

Establish a supportive network for discussing and sharing ideas, tools, and knowledge about data-driven teaching and research in education.

Promote Awareness and Integration of AI in Education

Advocate for the adoption of AI and NLP in education, showcasing its potential to transform teaching and learning practices in Morocco.

SenseCEFR

Context-Aware Vocabulary Profiler with Word Sense Disambiguation

An advanced open-source NLP profiler that assesses vocabulary level against the CEFR framework by disambiguating word senses directly in context rather than relying on isolated lemmas.
